## 🎨 Design Features

### Color Scheme
- **Primary Dark**: `#0f1419` (main background)
- **Secondary Dark**: `#1a1f2e` (cards)
- **Accent Red**: `#ff4444` (CTAs, highlights)
- **Accent Orange**: `#ff6b35` (hover states)
- **Text Light**: `#ffffff` (primary text)
- **Text Gray**: `#b0b0b0` (secondary text)

### Typography
- **System Fonts**: Poppins → Arial (native, fast loading)
- **Font Sizes**: Responsive, readable on all devices
- **Headings**: Clear hierarchy (H1 > H2 > H3 > H4)

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop**: 1200px+ (full navigation, two-column layouts)
- **Tablet**: 769px - 1199px (adjusted grid)
- **Mobile**: 768px and below (hamburger menu, single column)
- **Small Mobile**: 480px and below (compact layout)

### Animations
- **Fade-in**: Page sections appear on scroll
- **Slide-in**: Hero content slides in
- **Float**: Hero icons float up and down
- **Hover**: Cards lift on hover
- **Button**: Buttons scale and change color on hover
- **Smooth**: Scrolling is smooth throughout
